# Correction proposal: the Arc structural registry

**Status: PROPOSAL. No file in any existing repository has been modified.**
Generated by ArcAdvisor Release A on 2026-11-03 from deposited coordinates.

## 1. The accessions do not contain what is claimed of them

`SmallModularBioReactors.jl/src/molecular/ProteinPeptideRegistry.jl:69-78` and
`flow-cytometry-model-bridge/src/M7VisualizationBridge.jl:9072-9083` assert an
icosahedral T=4 capsid of 32.0 nm diameter, citing PDB **6GSE** and **4X3H**.

| Accession | What it actually is | Verified from |
|---|---|---|
| 6GSE | Rat Arc **capsid-domain monomer**, solution **NMR**, C1, 1 chain, 53,020 atoms (ensemble) | RCSB + local parse |
| 4X3H | Rat Arc **N-lobe** + Stargazin peptide, **X-ray 2.40 A**, 806 atoms, declared assembly 2 | RCSB + local parse |

Neither contains a capsid, an icosahedral assembly, or any T number. The
asserted `structure_class = :experimental_cryo_em_xray_structure` is wrong twice
over for 6GSE, which is neither cryo-EM nor X-ray. Every other entry in that
registry labels its method correctly; `:Arc` is the only one that does not.

## 2. Field-level errors in the `:Arc` registry entry

| Field | Current | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| `uniprot_or_pdb_id` | `"PDB:6GSE"` | A monomer domain cited as the source of capsid geometry |
| `structure_class` | `:experimental_cryo_em_xray_structure` | 6GSE is solution NMR |
| `stoichiometric_binding_sites_per_monomer` | `240` | 240 is the **subunit count of a T=4 shell**, not binding sites per monomer. Every other entry holds 1, 2 or 4 actual sites |
| `target_metal_or_cofactor` | `:RNA_capsid_self_assembly` | Names no ion or cofactor; every other entry does |

`test/biological/test_biological.jl:116,119` pins both the wrong accession and
the 240, so the error is currently load-bearing in the test suite.

**Architectural principle proposed:** never derive Arc monomer counts, binding
sites or particle counts from a triangulation number. N = 60T, so the 240 of a
T=4 shell is a subunit count. Keep the existing `StructureAndBindingProvenance`
guard and extend it to cover this case.

## 3. Measured values, from the deposited coordinates

Computed by rebuilding each deposited assembly from its own operators and
measuring. Lengths in nm; see `geometry/shells.json` for full output.

| Quantity | Repo asserts | **Measured** | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| dArc1 capsid outer diameter | 32.0 nm | **39.85 nm** (p99), 41.07 nm (max) | 6TAP, 300 chains rebuilt |
| dArc1 triangulation number | T = 4 | **300 subunits; 300/60 = 5, which is NOT a valid Caspar-Klug number.** Decomposes as T=4 (240) **+ 60** | 6TAP |
| dArc1 shell thickness | not stated | 4.39 nm | 6TAP |
| dArc1 radius of gyration | not stated | 17.55 nm | 6TAP |

The T=4 label therefore describes 240 of the 300 chains, not the assembly, and
is attached to accessions that contain no assembly at all.

## 4. Validation of the measuring pipeline

NwV (1OHF), a genuine non-enveloped T=4 ssRNA virus, measures **41.79 nm** outer
diameter here against a published mature-capsid diameter of 42.8 nm, and its
subunit count resolves to exactly 240 (T=4) once the beta/gamma cleavage is
accounted for (480 deposited chains, 2 chains per subunit). The pipeline
reproduces a published number it was not tuned to.

## 5. Narrative corrections

- Pastuzyn et al. 2018 *Cell* (mammalian Arc) and Ashley et al. 2018 *Cell*
  (dArc1) are the two landmark papers and were absent from the evidence table.
- Dodonova et al. 2019 *PNAS* (Ty3, PDB 6R24) is the correct ancestral Gag
  reference. It also establishes that retroviruses evolved **from** LTR
  retrotransposons, so "an ancient HIV-like virus infected us" inverts the
  inference.
- The Lee EV-targeting paper is eLife **2023**, not 2022.
- The 350-400 Mya insertion date requires a dated phylogeny and is currently
  unsupported.
- There is **no high-resolution mammalian Arc capsid structure**. Every
  icosahedral measurement above is *Drosophila* dArc1 or Ty3.
